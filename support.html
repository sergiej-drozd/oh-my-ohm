Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Profile tab and tap Restore purchases, or tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the Pro screen. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose Oh my Ohm and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged again. See Apple's guide.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is needed. Lessons and practice work offline. Purchases need a connection to the App Store.

Is my progress backed up?

Progress is stored on your device and is included in your iCloud or computer backups. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I turn off reminders?

Go to the Profile tab and switch off daily reminders, or turn off notifications for Oh my Ohm in iOS Settings.

What do "sparks" and "charge" mean?

Charge is how many mistakes you can make before taking a break (Pro has unlimited charge). Sparks are earned by playing and can be spent on hints, refills and streak freezes. You can also buy spark packs.
