Privacy Policy

Oh my Ohm: Learn Circuits

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Summary

Oh my Ohm does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no backend, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (XP, lessons, streaks, achievements, sparks), your settings and the optional name you enter are saved only in the app's storage on your device. We never receive this data. It may be included in your device backups (iCloud or computer), which Apple manages under its own privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Reminders

If you turn on daily reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No push service is used and nothing is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in Profile or in iOS Settings at any time.

Purchases

Subscriptions, the lifetime unlock and spark packs are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, name or Apple ID. The app only asks Apple whether a purchase is active so it can unlock Pro features. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app is designed for students and adults learning electronics. It does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the app and on this page with a new date.
